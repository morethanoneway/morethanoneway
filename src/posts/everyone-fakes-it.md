# The Moment I Realized Everyone Is Faking It (and That’s Okay)

There’s a moment most people have at some point in college — a quiet, almost embarrassing moment — where you look around the room and think:

**“Is everyone else just… doing better than me?”**

For me, that moment happened during a random class (many eons ago). I remember sitting there, staring at someone who always seemed put together. They answered questions effortlessly. They took notes like they were preparing to publish a textbook. They never looked stressed, even though the rest of us were unraveling like loose threads.

Meanwhile, I was fighting a losing battle with my deadlines, my self-doubt, and a calculator that may as well have been written in ancient Greek.

And I remember thinking:

**“How are they so on top of everything?”**
**“What’s wrong with me?”**

## The Shift

That feeling stuck with me… until one day I overheard that same “perfect” classmate quietly telling someone they were struggling.

Not just a little — a lot.
Behind the calm face and crisp notes was someone who felt just as overwhelmed, unsure, and stretched thin as the rest of us.

It hit me harder than I expected:

That feeling stuck with me… until one day I overheard that same “perfect” classmate quietly telling someone they were struggling.

Not just a little — a lot.
Behind the calm face and crisp notes was someone who felt just as overwhelmed, unsure, and stretched thin as the rest of us.

It hit me harder than I expected:

**No one has it together.**
**Everyone is just trying, guessing, improvising, or hoping they’re doing enough.**

Some people fake confidence.
Some fake calm.
Some fake knowing what they want to do with their life.
Most of us fake all three.

And suddenly I felt lighter — not because anything got easier, but because I realized I wasn’t behind. I wasn’t broken. I wasn’t “less than.”

I was just human.

## The Takeaway

If you’ve ever felt like you’re the only one lost… you’re not.
If you’ve ever thought everyone else got the manual… they didn’t.
If you’ve ever wondered why you can’t figure it all out… no one else has, either.

Some paths are straight.
Most zigzag.
All of them are valid.

So the next time you look around and think you’re the only one struggling, remember:
**It’s okay to feel lost sometimes.**
**You’re not behind.**
**You’re human.**
**YAnd that’s enough.**
